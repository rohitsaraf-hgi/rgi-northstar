# HG Sales Copilot — the AI-native way to operationalize HG data

*A context layer that turns HG's proprietary data and your first-party signals into surfaced insights and executed workflows — dramatically reducing time to value for GTM teams.*

---

## The problem worth solving

HG has always had the data. Firmographics, technographics, intent, spend, hiring signals — the ground truth on who buys what, at what install depth, with what intent.

The problem was never the data. It was **operationalizing** it. Getting from "here are 10,000 ranked accounts" to "here's the email you send to Sarah at JPMC on Tuesday morning" was work no dataset could do on its own. Sales teams got exports, dashboards, and reports — and then still had to figure out what to do with them.

AI-native changes that. For the first time, we can build a **context layer** that interprets HG's data specifically for each customer — and turns raw signal into surfaced insight, then into executed workflow.

---

## The context layer

Every customer configures Sales Copilot with three things:

- **What you sell.** Your offerings — products, categories, differentiators.
- **Who you sell to.** Your ICP — firmographic profile, buying committee, key personas.
- **What pain you solve.** The problems your product removes for your customers.

This context is the interpretive lens on everything below. Every account HG scores, every intent surge, every first-party event — evaluated *through* your ICP and your buying-persona map. Two customers looking at the same underlying data see two different sets of priority accounts, ranked differently, with different reasons why.

---

## Context → Insight → Action

### Insight — HG data × your first-party data, through your context

Sales Copilot unifies two sources at read time:

- **HG proprietary data** — firmographic, technographic, intent, spend, corporate hierarchy
- **Your first-party data** — CRM, sequences, engagement events, intent research, webinars, form fills, champion movement

Applied through your context, this produces *"why now"* insights per account: *"this account is worth your time today because [reason grounded in your ICP + your pain points], cited to sources."* Every insight arrives with a suggested next step.

Signals self-organize into categories a rep actually acts on — buying intent, website engagement, champion moves, firmographic shifts, CRM changes — ranked by relevance to *your* GTM motion. Not *"here's the data";* rather *"here's what matters, given what you sell and who you sell to."*

### Action — workflows tuned to your GTM motions

Every insight resolves to a workflow the copilot runs on the rep's behalf — tuned to how the customer sells: prospecting, competitive displacement, expansion, renewal defense, inbound intent capture. Some ship out of the box; some the customer composes in a visual builder.

Workflows execute across the target audience with human approval on any step that reaches outside the platform. Outcomes feed back into the context loop — sharpening the ranking, tuning the next play, closing the last-mile gap that reports could never close.

---

## Why this compounds

Because everything runs through one context layer, work never gets rebuilt.

- The ICP and persona definitions your RevOps team configures live once and propagate to every workbook, every play, every account view.
- The insights the copilot surfaces are the same insights the workflow acts on — no translation layer, no lost fidelity.
- Every play that runs overnight sharpens the context for the next play.

The rep spends less time gathering, more time deciding. The copilot spends its cycles doing what it's good at — running plays across every record in scope, drafting the outreach, and holding the approval queue for the rep's review.

---

## The outcome: dramatically reduced time to value

The traditional GTM stack sold data. Sales Copilot delivers **operationalized data** — HG's insights woven into the rep's day, driving actions the rep would otherwise have to reason from scratch every morning.

For a GTM team, that compresses time to value across every dimension:

- **Onboarding** — new reps land into a system that already knows their ICP, personas, and motion.
- **Daily execution** — insights and next steps come pre-formed, cited, and one click from action.
- **Play throughput** — workflows run across every record in scope with the rep only in the loop where their judgment matters.
- **Campaign iteration** — a new motion goes from "here's the audience filter" to "the copilot is running it" in an afternoon, not a quarter.

---

**The AI-native shift isn't the model in the middle. It's what finally lets HG's proprietary data have a context layer built for each customer — turning data into insight, insight into action, and action into outcomes that feed the next round of insight.**

**Sales Copilot is that layer.**
